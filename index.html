    Automatic depth

    The app estimates a depth map of the photo on your device (Depth Anything V2; ~27 MB model, downloaded once). Near parts get more disparity than far parts, so the subject stands out from the background. Focus plane = depth that stays at the base offset.

    Horizontal offset

    Uniform horizontal displacement between the left- and right-eye views (binocular disparity), in output pixels. It sets where the whole picture sits in depth; with automatic depth on, the per-pixel depth disparity is added to it.

    Vertical offset

    Large vertical disparity makes stereoscopic fusion uncomfortable. Keep it subtle (≤ 2 px).

    Fractional values are rendered with sub-pixel interpolation.

    Extra depth layers
    Layers (paint each in its colour)

    One finger / mouse paints; two fingers (or the wheel, middle-drag) zoom and pan. Painting a layer takes those pixels from the others; unpainted areas are background. Tick “still” for layers that should not move.

    Pattern

    Hidden shape

    Open the Sketch view to see the drawing: orange = outline, blue = feature lines, grey = body.

    Made for

    How to see it: hold the image close, look "through" it as if at something far away, then slowly move back until the two guide dots become three — the hidden shape appears. The shape is your photo's depth map (see the Depth view).
