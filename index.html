Horizontal offset

Horizontal displacement is the difference between the left- and right-eye views (binocular disparity). Larger values increase the perceived stereoscopic depth separation; measured in output pixels.

Vertical offset

Large vertical disparity makes stereoscopic fusion uncomfortable. Keep it subtle (≤ 2 px).

Fractional values are rendered with sub-pixel interpolation.
